A career built on operations and insight

I started in the U.S. Air Force, rebuilding a records program so that every office could find and trust its information. Property management followed from 2016 to 2023, in commercial office towers and in residential and student communities, where I carried the budgets and led the teams on site. Data-backed leasing and variance reporting were already part of that work, and as a Business Manager from 2023 I led a 16-person team across two offices, where pairing workflow improvements with data assessments earned my promotion to Business Intelligence Manager in 2024. As a Business Intelligence Manager I built the BI function on a governed data platform that unified seven source systems, and the AI-assisted workflows I designed on top of it handled routine operational work while decisions that need judgment stayed with people.
