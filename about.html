Background

Patrick Hancock

As a systems architect and operational strategist, I bridge the gap between enterprise data and high-stakes business execution. My work covers designing the architecture, authoring the functional specifications and validating the end-to-end performance of data and AI ecosystems. Having run operations firsthand, I don't just build systems for data's sake; they exist to solve bottlenecks and deliver trusted operational engines.

Most recently at masLabor, I advanced from Business Manager of the H-2A visa program to Business Intelligence Manager by using data diagnostics to solve operational friction. After securing executive buy-in to modernize the enterprise infrastructure, I became the sole architect and builder of the company's new data platform. Partnering with every department, I engineered their daily operational tools, including workload-optimized case routing, automated document ingestion pipelines, and a proprietary, agentic AI knowledge assistant.

My systems strategy is rooted in my background as an operations executive. Before masLabor, I served as General Manager for a flagship luxury housing property with The Scion Group, directed a federally regulated asset at Denstock, and built operational budgets for Class-A commercial properties. My career began in the United States Air Force, directing records and knowledge management programs that earned "Superior" recognition from Air Combat Command.
