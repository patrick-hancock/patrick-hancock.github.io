How it is measured

Policy
DOC-SLS-01 asks for a first call within 15 minutes of a lead arriving. The share is counted over leads that were contacted; never-contacted leads are counted separately.
Source
Tiles: SM-01 to SM-04, SM-10 and Q-LEAD-01 (DAX export). Distribution: core.fact_lead, net of duplicates; the build checks its medians and never-contacted shares against SM-03 and SM-04 for every store.
Queue
mart.app_lead_exception_queue: net leads with no touchpoint at all.
Flag rule
Ranks first and more than 2 standard deviations from the others. For front-end gross per deal, low is the signal.
