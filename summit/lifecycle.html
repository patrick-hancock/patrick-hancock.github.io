How it is measured

Rule
Retail buyers whose ownership began at least 183 days before 31 Oct 2026, with no service appointment in their first 183 days (1 minus FO-20).
Consent
Each buyer's current contact preference comes from core.customer_contact_preference. The queue says "Do not contact" where the customer opted out or no preference is on file.
Source
Tiles: FO-20 and Q-LIF-01 (DAX export). Queue: mart.app_first_service_queue; the build checks its row count by store against the Databricks run.
