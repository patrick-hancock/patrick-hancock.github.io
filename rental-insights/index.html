Rental Insights

Rental Insights turns public rent, income and housing-cost data into eight defined measures for US metro and micro areas. This page reports how typical rent has moved since 2015, how affordable rent is against income and the HUD Fair Market Rent, and where most renters are rent-burdened. Every figure is exported from the project's own tables, and a spot check recomputes a sample of them with independent SQL.
