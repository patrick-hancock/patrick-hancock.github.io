Q&A eval gallery

Forty test questions put to the project's question-answering tools, which may answer only from the project's own tables and definitions. Each card shows how one run handled one question: its status (answered, refused, needs clarification, no data or error) and whether that matched the expected answer. A card passes only when the status and every number match.

Data Provided by Zillow Group

Answers that quote typical rent (ZORI) or home value (ZHVI) use Zillow figures; affordability figures come from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey and HUD Fair Market Rents. Built from the project's eval results; not investment advice.
