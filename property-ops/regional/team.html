Methodology and data scope

Unfilled authorized positions = authorized headcount minus people on staff, by role, from the monthly staffing record (October 2026). The record has no approval, funding or interim-coverage field, so those are not shown. Vacancy duration counts consecutive months, up to this one, with a vacancy. Shared staff = people on staff minus full-time equivalents working at the property; 0 means everyone on staff works on site full time. Historical reviews keep their own periods, and their figures are withheld until reconciled. No staffing timeline is shown, because the dates and units of the historical series have not been validated.
