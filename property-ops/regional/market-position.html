Methodology and data scope

Occupancy is occupied units (beds at student communities) divided by all units, from the October 2026 rent roll. The market occupancy benchmark is 100 minus the market's vacancy rate for the same month; for Blacksburg, Farmville, Waynesboro no observed rate exists, so the benchmark is an estimate (proxy), marked at every point of use. Difference from market benchmark = occupancy minus the benchmark, in percentage points. The renewal rate is leases renewed divided by leases that reached a decision (renewed or moved out), for leases ending in the trailing 12 months to the report month; open leases are excluded. The peer renewal benchmark is not market data: it is the median renewal rate of other Kestrel communities, taken from the first tier that has peers (same market and type, then same type, then same market, then all others). With an even number of peers the lower middle value is used. When every peer in that tier is of a different community type there is no comparable peer, and the renewal difference is under review: Briar Spur Townhomes, Dovetail Court and Quarry Wren. Historical reviews keep their own periods. The Kettle Ridge renewal review averages January 2025 to October 2026 and is not compared with the snapshot figures.
