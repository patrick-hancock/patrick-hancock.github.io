Where does the leasing process lose prospects, and how quickly are new leads answered?

Funnel: trailing 12 months to October 2026. Response time: October 2026 snapshot.

Methodology and data scope

Funnel: trailing 12 months to October 2026. Stage rates divide 12-month totals (for example tours / leads); the regional rate pools all 14 communities. Leases are new (non-renewal) leases from the lease records, by lease start month; the counts are source counts, not back-calculated from rates. The portfolio median is the middle of the 14 community rates for each stage; the relative difference is (community rate minus median) divided by the median, in percent, not percentage points. The weakest stage is the most negative relative difference among the first three stages. Response time: October 2026 snapshot. Average hours from a lead to its first response, as reported monthly by the leasing system. The source does not state whether these are business or calendar hours or whether automated replies count. The regional figure is the average of the 14 community averages, shown to two decimals so that a community at 2.3 hours is not read as equal to it; a community with no value would be shown as not reported and left out of the average. Response time is not compared with a service-level target. Pre-leasing pace is reported only November to July for student communities, so October 2026 has no figure. Historical reviews keep their own periods; disputed figures from them are withheld until reconciled.
