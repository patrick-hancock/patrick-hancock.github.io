Methodology and data scope

Each repair category is compared with the portfolio median for that same category. This is a relative portfolio comparison, not a published service standard. For each community the portfolio comparison is the median of the other communities' medians for that category; with an even number of values the lower middle value is used. Medians are days to close for work orders opened in the month and closed; 52 of 130 community and category medians rest on 1 or 2 closed orders, so small differences are not meaningful. Open work orders are counted at month end, with no service target applied. Higher work-order volume is not poor service by itself: each unusual movement in reviews, complaints or work orders is checked against move-in volume, and the reviews (February 2024 to August 2026) keep their own dates. Make-ready on time is measured only for turns with a move-in target.
