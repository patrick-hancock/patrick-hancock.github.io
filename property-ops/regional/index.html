Methodology and data scope

The matrix shows one defined measure per dimension for each community, each with its own window: occupancy gap to the market benchmark (October 2026 snapshot), unfilled authorized positions (October 2026 staffing record), lead to lease (12 months to October 2026) and the change in resident sentiment (12 months to October 2026 against the 12 months before, as the difference of the two scores shown to two decimals). No status thresholds are set yet, so no cell carries a status or color; values show their sign and direction. Repairs & Service has no column because there is no single comparable repair measure across communities. No trend chart is shown, because the detail pages do not yet carry a consistent monthly series for these measures. Priorities name communities where current measures stand out; none states a cause, and historical reviews (Sumac Hollow, Briar Spur Townhomes) are not listed as current priorities.
