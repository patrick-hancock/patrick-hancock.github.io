Methodology and data scope

Each community's sentiment = review-weighted average star rating (1 to 5) over the 12 months to October 2026, compared with the 12 months before. The regional figure is the average of the 14 community scores, each community counted equally; it is not weighted by review count. Change is the difference of the two scores as shown, to two decimals. Negative reviews = share of reviews rated 1 or 2 stars over the 12 months, with the number of reviews beside it. Resident issues count reviews and complaints by main topic; each review or complaint is counted once, under its main topic. They are not compared on a shared colour scale, because most are complaints and the student communities count beds rather than units. Unanswered leasing enquiries are not ranked as a resident issue, because that figure is not supported by the data. Move-out survey results are not shown until their definition is confirmed.
