How this is measured. Covers all 14 communities. Spent vs plan is capital spent over the capital plan; every project in the data is finished, so there is one phase. The project list has no month, so spending does not change with the month. Renewal change is the community's renewal rate in October 2026 minus its rate in the month each project ended, averaged over its finished projects. Limit: the data has no capital-return finding type, so payback is shown as this renewal change, not as a return; the findings below are the two that name a capital-program effect.
