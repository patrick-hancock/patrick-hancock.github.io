How this is measured. Covers all 14 communities for October 2026. Expense share is expenses over income against the planned share. The split compares the last 12 months with the 12 before: the rise in insurance and taxes is portfolio-wide, the rest of the rise is site-controllable, the same definition the expense findings use. A community whose total expenses fell is left out of the split, and a line that fell counts as zero. The heatmap shows each line's share of income against a year earlier because the data holds no plan per line. Concessions are the month's concessions over full rent.
