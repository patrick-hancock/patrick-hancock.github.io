How this is measured. Covers all 14 communities for October 2026. NOI is year to date against the plan (the budget). Rent growth is the year-over-year change in effective rent minus the market's. Asking rent is the average asking rent on new leases against the market's average effective rent. Pricing call: raise or cut when asking rent is 9.7 or more points under or over market, hold otherwise or when the month has fewer than 3 new leases (too few to act on). The 9.7-point band is measured: half the middle spread (interquartile range) of asking rent against market over the last 12 months, 110 community-months with enough new leases (quartiles -7.0 and +12.4 points). Cards on this page are the NOI-gap findings; none names a cause.
